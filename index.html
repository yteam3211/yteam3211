Y-Team Training

This repository is dedicated to training new programmers on the Y-Team.

The curriculum now has a full map from Java basics through robot simulation, logging, and the FrcCatalyst team library.

Open the full course map

The curriculum is divided into these major chapters:

  1. The Basics
  2. WPILib and CTRE
  3. Sensors and Mechanisms
  4. Autonomous
  5. Simulation, AdvantageKit, and Team Library

If you are already proficient in Java, you may skip to Chapter 1.2.1 Git Start. "Proficiency" implies a strong understanding of Object-Oriented Programming (OOP), Interfaces, Runnables, Consumers, and Suppliers.

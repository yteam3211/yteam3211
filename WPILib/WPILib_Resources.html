WPILib Learning Resources

Now that you have VS Code installed, it is time to look at the official documentation and video guides that will help you write your first lines of code.

Official Documentation: Your First Robot Project

The WPILib documentation, often called "Docs", is the most important resource for any FRC programmer. Your first task is to read through the guide for creating a test drivetrain program.

Task: Go to the official guide and follow the steps for your preferred language: Java, C++, or Python.
Creating a Test Drivetrain Program

What you will learn there:

  • How to create a new project from a template.
  • How to define motor controllers in your code.
  • How to use joystick inputs to drive a robot base.

Video Series: Zero to Autonomous

If you prefer watching a visual breakdown of how a robot is programmed from scratch, the Zero to Autonomous series is the gold standard for FRC beginners.

Task: Watch videos 1 through 6 in the Zero to Autonomous series.
Zero to Autonomous Video Series

Why watch this?

  • It explains the Command-Based framework, which we use.
  • It covers everything from basic driving to advanced autonomous path following.
  • It shows real-world examples of how the code interacts with a physical robot.

Pro-Tip for New Programmers

Do not worry if you do not understand every line of code yet. The goal right now is to see the structure: where the motors are defined, where the movement logic lives, and how the robot listens to your commands.
